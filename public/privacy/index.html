Privacy at Playframe.

Updated 15 September 2026

This notice explains how Playframe uses information on playframe.qd.je. Contact us at Larion1@gmail.com about this notice or your data.

Optional analytics

We use Google Analytics only after you choose "Allow analytics". Until then, this site does not load the Google Analytics tag. Analytics helps us understand which templates, films and guides people find useful.

When you opt in, Google receives page visits, video starts and completions, clicks to Fab, demos, guides and YouTube, and browser and device information. Google also receives network information needed to deliver its service. We send page paths and limited campaign labels, remove other query parameters, and reduce referring URLs to their website origin. We do not measure email-link addresses.

Advertising consent remains denied. Google signals and advertising personalization are disabled in our tag. We do not use this implementation for advertising profiles.

Cookies and your choice

We remember your choice in this browser's local storage for 180 days. If you agree, Google Analytics can set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_K819TB5RLR, configured to last up to 180 days. Your browser may remove them sooner.

Use "Privacy choices" on any template, guide or studio page to change your choice. Choosing "No thanks" stops this implementation's analytics, removes its Google Analytics cookies for this website and reloads the page. It does not delete data already received by Google. This privacy page does not run analytics.

Hosting and external services

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers process connection information to serve pages. See GitHub's privacy statement. Videos played directly on this site are served as website files. Links to YouTube, Fab and Google Drive take you to those services, whose own privacy practices apply.

Google processes analytics information under its terms and may process it outside your country. Read how Google uses information from partner sites and Google's privacy policy.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use the information you provide to respond to your request. You can also contact us to ask about access to or deletion of information you have shared with Playframe.
