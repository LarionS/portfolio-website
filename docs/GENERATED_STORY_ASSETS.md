# Generated story assets

The following reconstruction backplates were created with Codex’s built-in Image Generation workflow on 27 July 2026. They are original fictional visualizations for confidential work; they are not client screenshots, documentary footage, or literal product interfaces.

Optimized WebP files are used at runtime. PNG masters are retained beside them.

## Clinical decision

**Runtime:** `assets/journey/worlds/story-first/clinical-decision-v2.webp`
**Master:** `assets/journey/worlds/story-first/clinical-decision-v2.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: immersive portfolio station background, 16:9 landscape
Primary request: a premium cinematic visualization of a high-stakes clinical simulation training moment, designed to instantly communicate “observe, decide, act, replay” without relying on text
Scene/backdrop: contemporary hospital simulation room at night, believable bed, patient simulator, realistic medical equipment and a glass observation/control room behind it
Subject: foreground gloved trainee hands reaching toward the patient while a vital-sign waveform visibly changes from stable cyan to urgent amber; another clinician reacts; a subtle ghosted sequence of three decision moments moves through the depth of the room
Style/medium: photorealistic cinematic production still with restrained near-future data-light accents integrated into the environment, not a UI mockup
Composition/framing: wide 16:9, immersive trainee point of view, strong depth, patient and hands as primary focal point, observation room and decision sequence clearly legible, no empty copy area required
Lighting/mood: clinical cyan and soft white with one controlled amber alert, serious, calm under pressure, premium film lighting
Materials/textures: real fabric, skin, silicone mannequin detail, brushed hospital equipment, slightly reflective glass
Constraints: no readable text, no logos, no brands, no floating rectangular UI cards, no split-screen, no gore, no watermark; make the action and consequence understandable from the image alone
Avoid: generic stock-photo pose, sci-fi hologram clutter, toy-like equipment, exaggerated red emergency lighting
```
