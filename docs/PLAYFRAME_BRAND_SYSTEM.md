# Playframe brand system

Date: 2026-08-28

## Strategic position

Playframe is a production Unreal Engine and VR systems studio for organizations that need people to practise difficult situations before they happen in the real world.

The brand is deliberately positioned away from two crowded categories:

- not an XR marketing-experience agency;
- not a generic software outsourcing company.

Playframe owns the difficult middle: multiplayer simulation, instructor control, physical hardware, wearables, haptics, telemetry, and the real-time world connecting them.

### Core proposition

**Immersive training. Engineered for reality.**

### Five-second explanation

**Unreal Engine & VR systems for high-stakes training.**

Multiplayer simulations, instructor control, live hardware and measurable outcomes.

### Audience

The primary audience is a decision-maker evaluating a serious training or immersive system: innovation leaders, training directors, operational teams, product owners, and technical partners in healthcare, defense, emergency response, and location-based entertainment.

They need to understand four things quickly:

1. Playframe builds production systems, not demos.
2. The work joins software, people, and physical hardware.
3. Playframe can handle complex, confidential, real-world deployments.
4. Starting a conversation is simple.

## Competitive research

The category has a persistent tradeoff between clarity and aspiration.

- [Dimension Studio](https://dimensionstudio.co/) is visually restrained and cinematic, but its broad positioning asks the work to explain the offer.
- [Magnopus](https://www.magnopus.com/) projects scale through ambitious case-study imagery and a work-first grid.
- [Simvra](https://simvra.com/) communicates its VR-training offer immediately and grounds it in equipment, procedures, assessment, and Unreal Engine.
- [Baal Studio](https://www.baalstudio.com/) uses cinematic Unreal imagery to establish atmosphere, though its first-screen message is less direct.
- [Reflex Arc](https://www.reflexarc.co.uk/) and [Another Reality Studio](https://anotherreality.studio/) position around broad immersive/spatial capability and production delivery.
- [Unreal Engine's Torch Technologies case study](https://www.unrealengine.com/spotlights/from-defense-to-healthcare-training-building-vr-on-a-common-framework?lang=en-US) reinforces the value of a shared systems core spanning defense and healthcare training.

The opportunity for Playframe is to combine Simvra-level comprehension with Dimension/Magnopus-level visual confidence, while owning a more specific idea: **the complete connected training system**.

### Category color audit

The current market already has clear color territories:

- Unreal Engine uses a black-and-white product shell with sky-blue actions.
- Strivr uses royal blue and cyan across its identity and conversion UI.
- Moth+Flame uses a dark field with a berry-red accent.
- Varjo, Magnopus, and Dimension rely heavily on monochrome and cinematic imagery.
- Simvra uses orange as its principal action color.

Acid lime was rejected after review. It is heavily overused by AI-generated creative-tech sites, has weak ownership for Playframe, and made the identity feel templated rather than authored.

Playframe uses a dense ultramarine instead. Blue is category-native, which helps enterprise trust, but this specific treatment deliberately avoids the market's cyan/SaaS shorthand: the hue is deeper, the background is blue-black rather than neutral charcoal, and the accent never becomes a gradient or ambient glow.

## Brand idea

### A frame becoming a world

The identity begins with the ordinary meaning of “frame”—a boundary, a viewport, a moment—and turns it into a spatial system. The mark uses two offset frame elements moving toward one another. Their negative space behaves like a portal and loosely suggests a forward-facing `P` without relying on a literal monogram.

The motion principle is simple: **frame → connect → reveal**.

It can appear in transitions, image crops, hover states, and the contact sequence without becoming a decorative gimmick.

## Visual system

### Primary palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blue-black Carbon | `#070913` | Primary background; authority and cinematic depth |
| Mineral White | `#F1F3F7` | Primary type and light surfaces; cool, clear, and less clinical than pure white |
| Playframe Ultramarine | `#5D76FF` | Logo, actions, focus, and live system states |
| Active Ultramarine | `#7890FF` | Hover and high-visibility interaction state |
| Deep Ultramarine | `#18245F` | Large color fields where the bright accent would become loud |
| Steel | `#9CA6B7` | Supporting copy and technical metadata |
| Carbon Soft | `#11162B` | Secondary dark surface and media separation |

The color balance is intentionally restrained: blue-black and mineral white carry roughly 95% of the interface; bright ultramarine is reserved for the mark, primary actions, indices, focus rings, and small active states. Full-width colored surfaces use Deep Ultramarine, never the bright accent.

For accessibility, Blue-black Carbon on Playframe Ultramarine has a contrast ratio of approximately `5.2:1`, and Mineral White on Deep Ultramarine is approximately `13:1`. Filled ultramarine buttons therefore use carbon text rather than white.

Do not use cyan, lime, blue-purple gradients, iridescence, or neon bloom. Those treatments collapse the identity back into generic XR/AI styling.

### Typography

- **Inter Tight Variable** — identity, display, headings, navigation, and UI. It is contemporary, highly legible, and compact enough for assertive editorial composition.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — system labels, timestamps, media captions, and small technical proof points.

No third typeface is needed. Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, tracking, and alignment.

### Photography and film

Every image must answer at least one of these questions:

- who is training;
- what system surrounds them;
- what physical hardware is connected;
- what virtual consequence is being rehearsed;
- what shipped experience exists.

The art direction is documentary-cinematic: real bodies, credible environments, controlled available light, clear physical-to-digital relationships, and no visible third-party branding. Avoid cyberpunk lighting, low-poly stand-ins, decorative holograms, fake interfaces, and anonymous headset close-ups.

### Layout

- Full-bleed media establishes the first impression.
- Copy occupies calm regions of an image and stays short.
- Each section has one job and one dominant visual.
- Editorial rules and chapter numbers provide order without a permanent HUD.
- Cards are avoided except where the content itself is an interactive media tile.

## Voice

### Characteristics

- exact, not breathless;
- confident, not inflated;
- concrete, not abstract;
- technically fluent, not jargon-heavy;
- human, not militarized.

### Vocabulary to prefer

`build`, `connect`, `train`, `control`, `respond`, `measure`, `deploy`, `real hardware`, `working system`, `production`.

### Vocabulary to avoid

`prototype`, `cutting-edge`, `revolutionary`, `metaverse`, `limitless`, `next-generation`, `immersive solutions`, and unsupported superlatives.

## Site narrative

1. **Identify** — Playframe builds Unreal Engine and VR systems for high-stakes training.
2. **Explain the system** — multiplayer, instructor control, physical hardware, and live data operate together.
3. **Prove range** — clinical, connected defense, and emergency-response training.
4. **Prove craft** — Hover the Edge and FlyboxVR show embodied interaction and shipped experience.
5. **Show product breadth** — Lighthouse, MoneyNest, and BiteSync demonstrate mobile product thinking without distracting from the core offer.
6. **Convert** — one invitation to discuss the system the client needs.

## Interaction system

- Hero media enters with a single controlled scale and exposure settle.
- Section copy and rules reveal only when the corresponding media is visible.
- Film starts as soon as its section enters the viewport and pauses off-screen.
- Media responds to pointer movement with very small depth, never enough to disturb reading.
- Navigation uses native scrolling and preserves every wheel/trackpad input.
- Reduced-motion mode removes transforms and autoplay motion while preserving all content.

## Success criteria

- The offer is understood in five seconds.
- The hero provides a recognisable human/system story, not generic VR imagery.
- No confidential client material is exposed.
- Scrolling is native, smooth, and reliable on Windows and macOS.
- The page remains useful without WebGL.
- Every primary CTA reaches the same frictionless project-enquiry path.
